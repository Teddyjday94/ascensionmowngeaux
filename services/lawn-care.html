Services / Lawn Care

Lawn care that holds up between visits.

Cutting, string trimming, edging, blowing, and herbicide application for residential and commercial properties across Ascension and Livingston Parishes.

Residential

A lawn you don't have to think about.

Weekly and biweekly plans built around how your grass actually grows through the season, not a fixed calendar. Every visit covers the cut, string trimming, edging, and blowing, with a $50 minimum.

We flag anything worth knowing, including bare patches, weeds, pests, or drainage trouble, instead of mowing past it.

Commercial

Grounds maintenance for properties that can't look neglected.

Office buildings, retail centers, and apartment complexes need routine mowing and seasonal treatment on a schedule that doesn't slip. We coordinate around your tenants and traffic, and scale crews to the size of the property.
