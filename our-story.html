Our Story

Eleven years, one crew philosophy.

Delivering reliable lawn care and landscaping to Prairieville, Gonzales, Baton Rouge, Livingston, and the surrounding parishes.

How It Started

One mower, a lot of word-of-mouth.

Ascension Mow N' Geaux was founded in 2015 on a simple idea: do the work well, show up when you say you will, and the rest takes care of itself. What started as residential mowing grew into a full lawn and landscape company, covering everything from weekly maintenance to full outdoor redesigns.

We still run on the same principle. Every property gets a crew that knows it, and every client gets a straight answer instead of a runaround.

Where We're Headed

More design work, more sustainable practices.

We're expanding into more specialized landscape projects, outdoor living spaces, hardscaping, and larger design builds, while keeping the maintenance side that got us started. Alongside that growth, we're leaning further into eco-friendly practices: smarter irrigation, native plant selections, and techniques that keep properties healthy long-term instead of just looking good for a season.
