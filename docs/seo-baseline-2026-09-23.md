## Competitive patterns observed

Businesses that surfaced consistently tended to have several of these elements:

- clear city and service wording in titles and headings
- specific descriptions of what is included in recurring service
- visible local proof such as address, reviews, years in business, licenses, or local organization listings
- detailed service pages instead of generic paragraphs
- FAQs that answer scheduling, scope, service area, and project questions
- dedicated local service-area signals without unrelated locations
